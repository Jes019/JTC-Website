Information collected

The quote form requests contact details, property locality and type, service preferences and concerns needed to review an enquiry. The public form should not be used to share alarm codes, key-safe codes, banking details or other sensitive access information.

Why information is used

Enquiry information is used to respond, assess service suitability, confirm availability, prepare a quotation and communicate about the requested service. It should not be used for unrelated marketing without an appropriate legal basis or consent.

Sharing and processors

Information may be handled by service providers used for website hosting, form delivery, email or customer administration. The exact providers and international transfer safeguards must be listed once integrations are selected.

Retention and rights

A documented retention period must be approved. Individuals may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the handling of their personal data, subject to applicable law.
