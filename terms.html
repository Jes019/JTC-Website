Plans and availability

Property care plans run on a yearly agreement with monthly pricing. They are not month-to-month cancellation products. Service availability and suitability must be confirmed before commencement. Proposal validity may be limited to a stated period.

Access and approvals

Access must be provided or authorised by the owner. Key holding can be included in the proposal. Major repairs are proposed before proceeding. Immediate action should only occur when authorised or reasonably required to address an urgent safety risk or prevent further damage.

Scope and professional work

  • Visual inspections are not structural surveys.
  • Appliance visual checks are not technical certifications.
  • Damage reports are not insurance-loss assessments.
  • JTC does not replace property insurance.
  • Licensed works are completed or coordinated through appropriately licensed professionals.
  • JTC provides coordination and reporting; it does not replace licensed experts where legally required.

Owner occupation

Where agreed, regular visits may be suspended while an owner is in residence. Annual service terms continue as set out in the accepted proposal.
