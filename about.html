Your Property. Our Priority.

Reliable property care, with a personal approach.

JTC Property Services exists to give owners a trusted local contact in Malta.

The purpose

Clear Information When You Cannot Be There

Owning a property while living abroad or travelling regularly can leave practical questions unanswered. JTC combines visual inspections, practical property support, cleaning and contractor coordination so owners can make informed decisions from wherever they are.

The focus is simple: communication, early action and transparency.

The JTC approach

  • Visit and visually check agreed areas
  • Photograph and document findings
  • Report urgent issues immediately
  • Recommend practical next actions
  • Coordinate approved support
  • Keep third-party costs visible
