Straight answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Which areas of Malta do you cover?

JTC Property Services operates across Malta. Availability and location-related requirements are confirmed before service begins.

Which prices are shown online?

The Basic, Standard and Premium yearly care plans show monthly prices inclusive of VAT. Additional services are assessed and included in a tailored proposal.

Do monthly care plans require a yearly agreement?

Yes. Property care plans are yearly care agreements with monthly pricing.

Do you provide a report after every inspection?

Scheduled visits include a written update and photographic reporting according to the selected plan.

Can you hold a key or let contractors in?

Yes. Key holding and contractor access can be included in your proposal, subject to agreed access and security arrangements.

Can you complete repairs yourself?

JTC can handle minor practical jobs that do not require a licensed specialist. Licensed or major work is coordinated and proposed separately.

Will work proceed without permission?

Major work and specialist repairs should not proceed without owner approval, except where immediate action is authorised or reasonably required to prevent an urgent safety risk or further damage.

Are materials included?

Materials, parts and third-party contractor charges are identified separately in the proposal or submitted for approval before proceeding.

Is every inspection a technical survey?

No. Standard visits are visual property checks. Specialist inspections, certifications and licensed works are separate services.

How do I start?

Send the property details, preferred inspection frequency and required services. JTC will confirm availability and prepare a suitable proposal.
