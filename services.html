Inspections & reporting

Know What Is Happening at the Property

JTC carries out visual condition checks of accessible areas, documents visible findings and sends a clear update. Urgent issues are reported immediately.

  • Scheduled property-care inspections
  • One-off property inspections
  • Post-tenant and post-guest inspections
  • Post-departure inspections
  • Visual damage condition reports with photographs
  • Meter readings where applicable
Include Inspection Services

During an agreed visit

  • Accessible rooms and visible surfaces
  • Doors, windows, shutters and security concerns
  • Visible leaks, damp, pests and damage
  • Agreed taps, lights and appliances
  • Photos, written summary and recommended actions

Visual condition reports are not structural surveys, insurance-loss assessments or specialist certifications.
